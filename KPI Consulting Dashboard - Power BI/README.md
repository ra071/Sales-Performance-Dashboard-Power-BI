# KPI Consulting Services – Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

End-to-End Business Intelligence Project

## Project Overview

This Power BI project showcases a complete sales analytics dashboard created by a fictional consulting company called KPI Consulting Services.
The goal of the dashboard is to provide decision-makers with clear, interactive, and data-driven insights into sales performance across multiple stores, products, customer segments, and time periods.

This project demonstrates my ability to build professional BI reporting solutions that align with real business needs such as performance monitoring, customer insights, and strategic planning.

## What I Did (Project Steps)

### 1. Data Understanding and Preparation

Imported raw sales data containing store details, product information, customer demographics, transactions, quantities, and dates.

Cleaned and transformed the dataset in Power Query by:

- Removing inconsistencies

- Creating date hierarchies

- Standardizing categorical fields

- Ensuring proper data types

### 2. Data Modelling

Built a star schema with fact and dimension tables to support accurate and fast reporting.

Created relationships between:

- Customers

- Products

- Sales

- Stores

- Date

### 3. DAX Measures Development

Created key business metrics including:

- Total Sales

- Total Quantity

- Average Spend per Transaction

- Number of Transactions

- Distinct Customers Purchased

- YoY comparisons and trend lines

These measures enabled deeper analysis across categories, demographics, and time.

### 4. Dashboard Design & Visualisation

Designed a clean, modern Power BI dashboard that includes:

🔹 KPI Cards (Summary Metrics)

- Total Sales: 14M

- Total Quantity: 8013

- Average Spend per Transaction: 6.32K

- Transactions: 2287

- Customers Purchased: 36

🔹 Time-Series Visuals

- Sales trends by year and month

- Customer purchase trends

Insights into seasonal peaks and dips

🔹 Performance by Category & Product

- Electronics generated 11.7M, dominating total sales

- MacBook alone contributed 5.4M, making it the top-selling product

🔹 Customer Demographics

- Sales by gender show balanced purchasing behavior (Female 7.3M vs Male 7.2M)

- Sales strongest among Age 30–40 (6.7M)

- Occupational insights highlight top segments like Engineers and Accountants.

🔹 Store Performance

- Costco and Best Buy lead with 3.9M and 3.8M respectively

5. User Interactivity

Added slicers for:

* Store Name

* Product Category

* Product Name

* Customer Name

* Gender

* Age Bucket

* Date Range

This allows dynamic analysis for targeted insights.

## Outcome & Insights

The dashboard produced several actionable insights:

### Key Outcomes

* Electronics dominate sales, contributing 11.7M (84%).

* Best-selling product: MacBook (5.4M).

* Distinct Customer base is small (36 customers) but high-value.

* Highest purchasing group: Age 30–40, contributing nearly 50% of total revenue.

* Costco and Best Buy outperform other stores.

* Sales trend shows fluctuations but a noticeable peak around Jan 2023.

### Business Value Delivered

* Helps executives quickly understand revenue drivers.

* Enables targeted marketing based on age, gender, and occupation.

* Allows store managers to benchmark performance.

* Supports better inventory and product planning.
